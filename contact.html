Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our strategy sessions and process

Is the strategy session really free?

Yes, completely free with no strings attached. We provide genuine value in every session because we believe in earning your trust through results, not sales pressure. Many businesses implement our recommendations on their own and see immediate improvements.

What happens during the 30-minute session?

We'll analyze your current digital presence, identify your biggest opportunities, discuss your specific goals, and create a prioritized action plan. You'll leave with actionable steps you can implement immediately, whether you work with us or not.

Will you try to sell me something?

No high-pressure sales tactics. Our approach is consultative - if we can help you, we'll explain how. If you're better off handling things internally or with someone else, we'll tell you that too. Our goal is to provide value and build long-term relationships.

What if my budget is on the lower end?

We work with businesses of all sizes. Even with smaller budgets, we can often identify high-impact, low-cost strategies that deliver significant results. We'll help you prioritize what matters most for your specific situation and budget.

Do I need to prepare anything?

We'll send you preparation materials after you book, but the main thing is to come with your goals and challenges in mind. Having access to your website, social media, or any current marketing materials during the call is helpful but not required.

How quickly can we get started?

Most projects can start within 1-2 weeks of signing the agreement. Rush jobs can often begin within 24-48 hours for an additional fee. Timeline depends on the specific services and your responsiveness to our requests for materials.
